# Record genuine contributions and adoption

After publication, keep a dated evidence log of merged pull requests, reviewed issues, releases, independent user reports and reproducible before/after outcomes. Use stable public URLs and distinguish your own work from others' contributions. Record measurement windows and how counts were obtained; preserve exported snapshots when a platform's analytics expire.

Do not treat downloads, stars or your own test installations as proof of commercial impact. Ask permission before quoting identifiable users, and retain only necessary information. Never invent supporters, endorsements or contribution history.

For a technical portfolio, describe the problem, your specific design/implementation contribution, validation and independently observed outcomes. This evidence may be useful for future applications, but this project makes no claim about immigration eligibility, endorsement or the sufficiency of any evidence. Consult the current official requirements for the intended application when preparing it.
