# Thinking standard

Start with the real decision and its output contract. Product facts come from approved decisions and evidenced repository behavior; planned does not mean live. No generic sample establishes a fact about this project.

Gather facts and label them `verified` with source, `assumption` with rationale, or `unknown`. For meaningful decisions compare distinct options, challenge the recommendation, then state a preferred action, its cost/risk, a measurable outcome and the first step. Do not pad a trivial mechanical task with unnecessary alternatives.

## Report shape

1. `unverified:` facts not checked, or `none` with justification.
2. Answer-first summary, two or three lines.
3. Recommendation, why, cost/risk, measurement and first step.
4. Options table when helpful, no more than four rows.
5. Assumptions, unknowns and sources with access dates for online claims.

Use the task's single Hand-off block as the canonical resume state. The output document's summary is not a second competing hand-off. Return result, open decision and output path to PM. Do not invent customers, adoption, legal certainty, professional authority or security guarantees. Do not publish or contact outsiders without explicit approval.
