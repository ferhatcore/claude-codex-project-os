# Optional advanced review queue

Disabled by default. If enabled, map the review tier, obtain O6 approval and use a bounded brief. The strongest available daily model is acceptable when the project policy permits it; do not assume a special premium model exists.

| ID | Proposed job | Inputs | Output | Approval | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-001 | Review project rules and dispatch policy after bootstrap | Confirmed entry/rules/config only | Scope in a brief before dispatch | pending | proposed |

The first review is a proposal, not an executed certification. Add risk-based repository/contract reviews as needed. Required-before-final policy is opt-in in configuration; if enabled, draft rules remain draft until the documented review and human adoption are complete.
